Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 27, 2026

Short version: STARLIGHT BBS does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It works entirely offline, and everything it saves stays on your device.

1. Overview

STARLIGHT BBS ("the App") is developed and maintained by Luca Manzoni ("I", "me", "my"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. By using STARLIGHT BBS you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

STARLIGHT BBS is designed with privacy as a default. The App does not:

  • Connect to the internet or to any server
  • Collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier
  • Require an account
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services that transmit data off-device
  • Serve advertisements or build advertising profiles
  • Share any data with third parties

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

So you can pick up where you left off, the App saves your progress on your device: the chapter you've reached, what you've read, the handle you chose, your sound setting, and the message you write during the game. This is stored in the App's local storage on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the App deletes it.

Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. I have no access to your backups.

4. Purchase

STARLIGHT BBS is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. I do not receive or store any payment or billing information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

STARLIGHT BBS does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The App does not collect personal data from any user, regardless of age.
