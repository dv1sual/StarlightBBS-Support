# STARLIGHT BBS Support

STARLIGHT BBS is a short narrative game for iPhone. It's 31 December 2004, and a small bulletin board is shutting down at midnight. You dial in one last time and read what everyone left behind. It takes about an hour.

## Report a Bug or Ask a Question

[Open an issue](https://github.com/dv1sual/StarlightBBS-Support/issues) and use the template below. Please keep story details out of the issue title, so other players don't see them.

```
App Version:
iOS Version:
Device Model:
Chapter (if you know it):

Description:
[Please describe the issue here]

Steps to Reproduce:
1.
2.
3.

Screenshot:
[Attach a screenshot if applicable]
```

You can also email [luca@lucamanzoni.com](mailto:luca@lucamanzoni.com).

## Download

STARLIGHT BBS is available on the App Store.

## Privacy

STARLIGHT BBS does not collect or share any data. It works fully offline, and your progress stays on your device. No account is required. [Read the privacy policy](https://dv1sual.github.io/StarlightBBS-Support/privacy-policy.html).
